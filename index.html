۝٦١ إِنَّ رَبِّي قَرِيبٌ مُّجِيبٌ

“Indeed, my Lord is near and responsive.” — Surah Hud: 61

A comforting sanctuary for the nocturnal soul through two intimate divine names: Al-Qareeb (The Ever-Near) and Al-Mujeeb (The One who responds). Al-Qareeb is closer to you than your jugular vein—present in your quietest moments and aware of unspoken sighs before they even form into words. Al-Mujeeb guarantees that no sincere yearning is ever overlooked or lost in the cosmos. Whenever you call out in solitude, divine mercy and solace are already answering.
